Step 01 · Pause

Give emotions some breathing room

Take a little time before sending a message. Write what you feel privately first, and avoid texting repeatedly while upset.

🪞
Step 02 · Reflect

Understand what went wrong

Separate facts from assumptions. Ask yourself what you could own, what hurt you, and what would need to change.

💌
Step 03 · Prepare

Find honest, respectful words

Use the message helper below to draft something genuine. Apologize for your actions without excuses or demands.

🫶
Step 04 · Communicate

Ask whether they're open to talking

Invite a calm conversation, listen without interrupting, and respect a “no” or a request for space.

🌱
Step 05 · Grow

Agree on realistic next steps

If both of you want to try again, discuss boundaries and specific changes. Trust is rebuilt through consistent actions over time.

⌛

✦

A little self-reflection

Relationship Check-in

This is not a scientific compatibility test. It's a gentle way to reflect on communication, respect, and readiness.

How are things feeling right now? 🫶

Choose the answer that feels closest to your situation. There are no “perfect” answers.

1. Can both of you speak honestly without fear?

2. Are both people willing to listen and take responsibility?

3. Are each other's boundaries respected?

4. What do you most want right now?

🔒
Your privacy & the limits of this demo

This single-file demo does not send your answers or message drafts to a server. The mission checklist is saved locally in this browser. Real AI-generated replies, secure accounts, and live chat between people require backend services, appropriate security, and privacy safeguards. Don't use this prototype to store sensitive information.
